Solmarina — an open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, built from scratch for make.fun's "Make GTA 6" bounty. A sun-soaked coastal city to explore on foot and by car: traffic that yields and pedestrians that flee (and jog), cars to steal, a wanted level with police who keep up at full throttle, seven story jobs for the fixer Mara Voss — one of them a race against the clock — combat, a horn, seagulls over the beach, a lighthouse and a pier, a HUD with a minimap, shops, and a save that restores money, job, kit, health and position. Everything is original — the city, the names, the story, the art and the sound (synthesised, no audio files). Nothing from Rockstar Games or any other commercial game.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. No build step, no network requests — the game is plain static files under `game/`.

Controls: WASD walk & drive · E enter / exit car · F punch · mouse aim, click shoot · Shift run · Space handbrake · H honk · J next job · P pause · M mute.
